1. What is Tanssi Network?
Tanssi Network is a modular infrastructure protocol within the Polkadot ecosystem. It enables developers to deploy application-specific blockchains, called "ContainerChains," in minutes. Built on Substrate, it simplifies blockchain development by automating processes like validator orchestration, RPC endpoints, and decentralized sequencing, making it ideal for DeFi, RWA, and fintech projects.
2. What are ContainerChains?
ContainerChains are customizable blockchains deployed via Tanssi Network. They leverage Polkadot's shared security and interoperability while allowing developers to tailor them for specific use cases, such as DeFi platforms, tokenized assets, or gaming ecosystems.
3. What is the $TANSSI token used for?
The $TANSSI token is used for:
  • Paying network fees
  • Staking to secure the network
  • Governance voting
  • Bridging assets across chains
  • Earning ecosystem rewards
It exists as both an ERC-20 token on Ethereum and a native Substrate token, interchangeable at a 1:1 ratio.
4. How does Tanssi ensure security?
Tanssi integrates with Symbiotic, providing over $350 million in economic security through Ethereum-backed restaking. It also uses Polkadot's shared security model, ensuring robust protection for all ContainerChains.
5. What makes Tanssi different from other blockchain platforms?
Tanssi simplifies blockchain deployment by automating complex infrastructure tasks (e.g., validators, explorers, wallets). It's permissionless, interoperable with Polkadot and Ethereum, and significantly reduces development time from months to minutes.
6. Who can use Tanssi Network?
Developers, startups, and enterprises building DeFi, RWA, gaming, or other Web3 applications can use Tanssi. Its permissionless nature allows anyone to deploy a ContainerChain without gatekeepers.
7. What are some projects built on Tanssi?
As of October 2025, projects like Decentral (RWA platform), BEEFY, Gavun Wud, and Lumia have launched ContainerChains on Tanssi. More are expected as the ecosystem grows.
8. How does Tanssi integrate with Ethereum?
Tanssi supports Ethereum through bridges and integrates with Symbiotic for restaking. The $TANSSI token's ERC-20 version allows seamless interaction with Ethereum-based applications.
9. When was Tanssi Network's mainnet launched?
Tanssi's mainnet launched in July 2025, enabling developers to deploy ContainerChains and access its full feature set.
10. How can developers get started with Tanssi?
Developers can visit tanssi.network to access documentation, join the permissionless network, and deploy ContainerChains. Tanssi also offers campaigns like "Let's Forkin' Dance" for funding and support.
11. What is the role of the Tanssi Foundation?
The Tanssi Foundation manages the Tanssi Network, ensuring its development, governance, and ecosystem growth. It was founded by Moondance Labs, the creators of Tanssi.
12. How can I participate in the Tanssi ecosystem?
You can:
  • Stake $TANSSI tokens to secure the network and earn rewards.
  • Deploy a ContainerChain as a developer.
  • Participate in governance voting.
  • Join community campaigns or airdrops (37% of $TANSSI supply is allocated for community rewards).
13. Where can I find more information about Tanssi?
Visit the official website at tanssi.network or check Tanssi's social media channels for updates. You can also explore Polkadot's ecosystem documentation for related insights.
